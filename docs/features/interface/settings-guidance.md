# Optional settings guidance

The optional Guide and Explain entries appear immediately above their exact controls. Guidance remains closed until requested. The original control remains usable and owns the real validation, locks, authentication, confirmation, history and action. Back retains the live draft; Cancel stops guidance and does not claim to undo completed changes.

## Delivery boundary

This is source-level settings integration and focused component/consumer evidence. Native and browser interaction, full search-builder guidance, all nested editor controls, read-only element explanations and per-click captures remain unverified. The inventory is not a product-wide completion claim.
