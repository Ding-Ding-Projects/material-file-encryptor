# Code of conduct

This project is a place to work on software together. Everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, is expected to follow these commitments.

## Our commitments

- Treat people with respect and assume good intent. Disagree with an idea, not with the person who offered it.
- Be specific and constructive in review. Say what you saw, why it matters and what would help.
- Welcome people of every background, experience level, language and ability. Plain wording, English and Cantonese, and accessible communication are part of the project's purpose.
- Respect privacy. Do not share another person's personal information, and do not paste credentials, keys or private files into any public space.
- Be honest about what you tested and what you did not. Do not present unverified work as verified.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, discriminatory language or sexualized attention.
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption of discussions, or publishing someone's private information.
- Attempts to trick, pressure or manipulate other contributors or maintainers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, close threads, and restrict participation when behavior breaks these commitments. To report a concern, contact a maintainer through GitHub. If the concern involves a maintainer, ask another maintainer to handle it. Reports are handled with discretion, and the person reporting will not be penalized for raising a concern in good faith.

This policy applies in all project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
